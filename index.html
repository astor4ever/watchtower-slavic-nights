How it plays

Short nights. Big decisions.

Your tower fires on its own. You choose what makes it stronger – and when to call on the old gods.

Survive the night

Thirty waves of drowners, strzygas, werewolves and leshies pour from the trees. Upgrade damage, range and HP as silver drops.

Stack your runes

At dusk and every three waves pick 1 of 3 runes. Fire, frost-herbs, criticals, extra arrows – they stack, so every run is different.

Call the old gods

Strike with Perun's Thunder, raise Mokosh's Shield, unleash Stribog's Gale. Timing a power right turns a lost night into dawn.

Grow the stronghold

Between nights build up the forge, palisade, herbalist's hut and shrine – and watch your village rise. It even gathers silver while you're away.

The night

Every wave hits harder. So do you.

Watchtower is a roguelite tower defense played with a single thumb. Runs last a few minutes, decisions come every few seconds, and nothing waits on timers.

  • Speed the night up ×2 – or switch on Auto-battle and watch
  • Rune mastery carries over: your favourites get stronger for good
  • Daily quests, achievements and a login streak in the stronghold
At the stroke of Midnight

Eight circles. Eight legends.

Reach dawn to unlock the next circle of night – stronger creatures, new enemies, bigger rewards, and a new boss waiting at wave 30.

Circle I

The Zmey

Takes to the sky when wounded, circles the tower and breathes fire.

Circle II

Baba Yaga

Vanishes and reappears, summons will-o'-wisps, goes berserk.

Circle III

Marzanna

Her frost slows your tower. Wounded, she raises an ice shield.

Circle IV

Great Likho

Snuffs out your runes from afar and splits into three.

Circle V

Veles

Lord of the underworld. A renewing shield and a storm of lightning.

Circle VI

Planetnik

The storm-bringer. Rides the clouds, calls strzygas and rains hail.

Circle VII

Koschei

The Deathless. His shield keeps growing back – and his death hides in a needle.

Circle VIII

Chernobog

The black god. Darkness shrinks your range, curses your tower and swallows the night.

Questions

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The whole game is free to play – in the browser and on Android.

Which platforms?

Any modern browser (desktop or phone) and Android 7+. iOS may come later.

Which languages?

English, Polish, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, German, French, Italian, Russian, Turkish and Indonesian. Change it anytime in Settings.

Does it work offline?

The Android version plays fully offline. The browser version needs a connection to load, then runs locally – your progress is saved in that browser.

Are there ads or purchases?

On Android there are optional rewarded ads – you choose to watch one to revive the tower, reroll runes or double the night's rewards. No banners, no forced ads. Optional purchases (amber, a starter pack, a 30-day pass, no ads) only buy convenience and looks, never tower strength. The browser version has no ads or purchases.

What data do you collect?

Our own analytics collects only anonymous gameplay events (like "night started" or "wave reached") with a random install ID, to balance the game – no names, emails or location. On Android, ads are served by Google AdMob, which asks for your consent in the EU and may use the advertising ID. Details in the privacy policy.
